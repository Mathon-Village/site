Like my neighbours Martin and Debbie at Dolefield Cottage, I have quite a lot of information about the Laytons since, because they owned Bank Farm, they also owned what is now our house but was then the main threshing barn.  That said, most of my material came from June Harwood (whom I met at Herefordshire Record Office some years ago) and is broadly similar to the stuff she has now put on the web so there may not be much that you have not already discovered.
